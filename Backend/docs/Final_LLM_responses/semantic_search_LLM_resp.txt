The French Revolution began in 1789 when France, a territorial state ruled by an absolute monarch, shifted sovereignty from the king to the people. The revolution declared that the nation was made up of French citizens who would shape its destiny. Revolutionaries introduced ideas of *la patrie* and *le citoyen*, fostering a collective identity. They replaced the royal flag with the tricolour, abolished regional customs, and created a centralised system of uniform laws and weights. The new National Assembly, elected by active citizens, proclaimed a republic, granted suffrage to adult males, and established national workshops to provide employment. Language, popular traditions, and print culture—pamphlets and cartoons—helped spread these ideas and mobilise the populace. How do you think the new sense of citizenship might have changed everyday life for ordinary French people?
